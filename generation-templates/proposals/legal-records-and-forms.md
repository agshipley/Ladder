Tiles: LEGAL-01 (corporate filings), LEGAL-06 (I-9 / offer letters / classification), COMP-02
(insurance schedule). **These are RECORDS of events or EXECUTED instruments — a generated record
is fabrication of a legal fact (LEGAL-02 doctrine generalized).**

## RULING NEEDED (before any authoring)
Confirm the generation posture per sub-tile:
- **LEGAL-01** — cert of incorporation, bylaws, EIN, **DE franchise-tax/annual-report payment
  record**. Proposed: GENERATION-EXCLUDED for the records (a franchise-tax payment record cannot be
  generated); TEMPLATE-ONLY blank forms + a filing/deadline CHECKLIST are the most Ladder may produce.

- **COMP-02** — schedule of EXECUTED insurance policies. Proposed: GENERATION-EXCLUDED for the
  policies/binding record; only a BLANK policy-schedule TEMPLATE (carrier/type/limits/period columns)
  is generable, populated by the operator.
