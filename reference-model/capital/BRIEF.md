## 0. Derivation notes
**Frame:** the category is organized on the function's own operating loop — plan capital → raise → administer → relate/report → next raise — each cycle compounding (cleaner records and warmer relationships lower the next raise's cost). The v1 draft's scrutiny-timeline spine (pre/post-term-sheet), inherited from the rejected BG framework, is corrected here; DD lists are demoted to the corroborating demand-side rung.

**Regime weighting (mixed polarity, per subcategory):** the founder/operator capital-management discipline is PRIMARY for CAP-01, CAP-06, CAP-07 (practitioner canon owns their essence; sourced at lineage grade). The tax-administrative and corporate-law regimes are PRIMARY for CAP-02/03/04/05 administration content. Scrutiny regimes corroborate throughout. Securities-law exemption compliance is Legal's per the Legal-run E-2 precedent (legal/BRIEF.md §4–5): a flagged standing requirement; filing records are not scored artifacts; amounts are never collected.

**Sprint-L rationale:** the concept doc names fundraising/IR as the first leverage-loop generalization target after GTM. The loop's prerequisites are this category's operating artifacts — a capital plan (strategy input), an investor pipeline (CRM analog), the cap table as system of record, an update practice (send arm). A diligence-shaped Capital category gives Sprint L nothing to automate; a function-shaped one is the loop's parts inventory.

**Genre ladder result:** the statutory rung exists and is load-bearing for administration subcategories only (no statute governs how much to raise). The model-document rung exists twice over: instrument forms (NVCA suite, YC SAFE) and the domain's canonical book (Venture Deals, 4th ed. 2019 — the closest thing capital strategy has to a maintained model text). Named-practitioner canon is the load-bearing rung for CAP-01/06/07 by design. No Capital strategy criterion is unconditional.

**Provenance honesty:** Capital remains externally uncorroborated as a top-level category (TAXONOMY §3); tier-hood is venture-context expert-authored. The content case is corroborated from both directions — scrutiny frames treat capitalization as a first-class section; the operator canon treats capital management as a named founder discipline with its own literature. Placement stays operator judgment.

## 2. Authority set

### Named-practitioner operator canon — three lineages + convergence rows
- LINEAGE PG — Graham/YC. "How to Raise Money" (2013, paulgraham.com, primary text, retrieved 2026-07-09): the three-phase capital trajectory; fundraising as a discrete phase with external-constraint rules; the raise sized to reach the next milestone. "Default Alive or Default Dead?" (2015): the solvency frame — know at all times whether current growth and burn reach profitability before cash-out; "explicitly separate facts from hopes." Corroborators (counted with PG, not independent): YC partner guidance; NYU Entrepreneurial Institute / PipelineRoad / Capwave restatements.
- LINEAGE F — Feld/Mendelson. Venture Deals: Be Smarter Than Your Lawyer and Venture Capitalist (Wiley, 4th ed., 2019; Foundry Group/Techstars provenance; retrieved 2026-07-09). The economics-vs-control decomposition of every financing term; term-sheet anatomy (liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, the option-pool shuffle — pre/post-money pool expansion as negotiated dilution); process discipline (parallel conversations, pacing offers to land together; next-round performance risk of peak terms); diligence both directions (reference the investor's portfolio founders).
- LINEAGE S — Suster/Upfront. "Invest in Lines, Not Dots" (Both Sides of the Table, 2010, primary retrieved 2026-07-09): investor decisions ride on trajectories observed over time; meet investors before raising, state intended progress, deliver the update that proves the slope. Independent corroborators: SpringTime Ventures (ABF framing), Great North Ventures (monthly progress updates to prospects), Hodgkinson/SignalRank (2025).
- Convergence row — raise sizing: 18–24 months of runway to a clean, externally verifiable milestone, plus a fundraising buffer (~3–6 months, the raise's own duration); independently converged across ≥5 sources (NYU 2025, PipelineRoad, Capwave, Pickard, GoingVC). Context annotation.
- Convergence row — seed dilution norms: ~10–20% per round; YC ≤15% guidance; VC entry-ownership targets 10–15% reverse-engineered from fund math (GoingVC). Context annotation; weight-marked (sources include content-marketing grade).

## 4. Conflicts (ruled)
C-1 — Update cadence: monthly-at-seed practitioner convergence (Visible, Pitchwise, OpStart, Carta-adjacent; Lemkin 1% rule) vs an investor-authored quarterly counterview; universal convergence that consistency outranks frequency. RULED: score defined cadence + consistent-send evidence; the monthly norm is a dated annotation (B-12).
C-2 — SAFE closing as 409A material event: BPM (2026-07: yes where terms meaningfully move the pro-forma cap table) vs Exact Partners (typically no). The statutory condition is fact-based. RULED: score the process — a documented material-event assessment tied to each grant cycle — never a fixed trigger taxonomy. Operator rule-of-thumb recorded as CAP-04 context annotation.
C-3 — Discrete-phase vs always-be-fundraising: PG (fundraising is a bounded phase; minimize and return to building) vs S-lineage/ABF (continuous cultivation). Both converge that updates flow between rounds; they diverge on intensity. RULED: score the existence of the relationship record and between-rounds touch evidence; doctrine and intensity are context. A company running PG-discrete with a maintained investor list and updates is complete; one with neither artifact is thin regardless of professed doctrine.
