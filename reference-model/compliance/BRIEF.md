## 0. Category-semantics rules (operator-ruled, 2026-07-09)

1. WHOLE-ARTIFACT DETECTION GRAIN (operator ruling, this run). Ladder is an
   operator-focused product, not a compliance product. This category detects
   compliance artifacts at the whole-artifact grain: a SOC 2 report is ONE
   detection object (exists / in progress / not triggered), never a
   decomposition into its criteria or controls. An ISO 27001 or ISO
   42001-class certificate is one object each. The reference model carries
   NO control catalog. Where a required-artifact list exists, it is the
   demand instrument's list — the received security questionnaire, the
   executed contract clause, the financing covenant — read from the corpus.
   The model never substitutes its own checklist for the demand's. This rule
   binds the classifier (Sprint Ma Task 2), the board rendering, and the
   Sec. 9E audit's treatment of this category.
2. Detection-and-referral posture (TAXONOMY Sec. 1 mandate, B-27): this
   category refers outward and never rebuilds compliance frameworks.
   Settled boundaries (B-5, B-18, B-26, B-27, B-28, B-32) were applied, not
   relitigated; no boundary challenges arose.
3. Purpose (customer-defense, sibling to the AI Operations statement): the
   tile answers "are we currently sellable to an enterprise buyer, and what
   is the outside world contractually demanding of us right now?"

## 1. Polarity and genre profile (Sec. 2A pre-steps, executed)

Polarity: scrutiny-primary — the inverse of the operating categories.
Standards bodies anchor what the artifacts ARE; the DEMAND SIDE (enterprise
security reviews, procurement requirements, financing covenants) is the
primary rung and anchors WHEN absence is a gap; insurance market practice
covers the B-5/B-26 slice; the statutory rung is largely absent BY DESIGN
(operating-law instruments route to Legal per B-27; the one statutory
adjacency, state workers'-compensation mandates, is Legal's operating-law
record — only the policy artifact is detected here); practitioner canon
corroborates only.

No-direct-canon note: the detection POSTURE (thin, referral-only) is a
Ladder design decision with no external canon; the detection OBJECTS are
canon-anchored (AICPA TSC, ISO, NVCA, SIG). Roster operator-designed;
criteria authority-backed.

domain: GRC compliance-automation content marketing (Vanta/Drata/

not survive the conclusion that certification is premature. Claims from

## 4. Trigger frame (stage honesty; governs all subcategories)

At Band 1, certifications are usually not yet held; their absence is
AFFIRMATIVELY NOT A GAP until a corpus-verifiable trigger exists. Trigger
classes, each readable from an owned record elsewhere in the corpus:

  T-A  a received enterprise security questionnaire or review request
       (the artifact or its sales-thread record — GTM/corpus evidence)
  T-B  an executed contract clause requiring certification, coverage, or a
       COI (Legal's executed-contract record)
  T-C  an executed financing covenant (Legal closing set / Capital record;
       e.g. the NVCA IRA D&O covenant)
  T-D  statutory employee threshold (workers' comp — Legal's operating-law
       record)
  T-E  a declared regulated-buyer or enterprise segment in the ICP (GTM-01)
       — the one forward-looking trigger, scored soft

B-32 inversion, held without contradiction: compliance artifacts are
detection OBJECTS here, and their absence pre-trigger renders no gap.
Post-trigger the tile scores whether the DEMANDED artifacts exist — never
whether the company adopted a vendor's default program. Pre-trigger
rendering is a TRIGGER REGISTRY (live covenants, COI clauses, review
requests, statutory thresholds — with pointers to the owning records),
never a wall of red or of n/a. Board-build note (Sprint Ma Task 4): the
E-1 keep ruling is conditioned on this rendering.
