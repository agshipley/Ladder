## 2c. LLC variant table (entity-form condition)

Band-1 venture-backed reality: the DE C-corp is the overwhelming norm, and an LLC instance is itself worth a non-scored annotation (venture financing typically forces conversion). Where the corpus shows an LLC, the classifier substitutes: operating agreement ↔ charter + bylaws (LEGAL-01(a)/(b)); member/manager consents ↔ board/stockholder consents (LEGAL-02 — the DE LLC Act defers to the operating agreement's consent mechanics, so LEGAL-02(b)'s §228 mechanics do not apply; authorization-traceability per C-1 still does); DE annual LLC tax, $300 due June 1, no annual report ↔ the March 1 report-plus-franchise-tax criterion (verified: corp.delaware.gov, same retrieval); NVCA closing-set expectations in LEGAL-03(b) render n/a (LLC financings are bespoke — documented-n/a, never red); NY foreign-qualification adds the **publication requirement** (LLC-only) and the NY LLC Transparency Act attestation/reporting overlay to LEGAL-01(e).
