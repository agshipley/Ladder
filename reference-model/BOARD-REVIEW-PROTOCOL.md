Board verdict-review protocol — bins v2.2
Status: v2.2 (2026-07-15, operator-ruled)
Governs: operator review of board runs; bin citations in
DECOMPOSITION-BACKLOG and criterion-amendment entries.

- **Bin 1 — corpus right, world wrong.** Verdict faithful to what
  was ingested, but the operator knows the company had the thing
  (email, portal, at counsel, never made the corpus). ->
  Ingestion-scope gap; Sprint M punch list.
  Bin 1 sub-classes: **1-i — the document exists but never made the
  corpus** (skipped, failed, or never handed over); remedy: pull it
  in. **1-ii — the evidence isn't a document at all** (code
  repositories, community activity, third-party operating systems);
  remedy: an ingestion-scope decision, not a retrieval task.
- **Bin 2 — evidence doesn't support the verdict.** Cited chunks
  don't say what the state claims, or retrieval grabbed the wrong
  material. -> Retrieval/judge defect; the serious class.
- **Bin 3 — right state, wrong reasoning.** Color's fine; the
  rationale or missing-elements list is off. -> Criterion prose or
  closure gap.
- **Bin 4a — bar wrong.** Seeing the criterion applied, the
  requirement itself is miscalibrated for Band 1. -> Criterion
  amendment; touched fixtures re-ruled.
- **Bin 4b — grain wrong.** The bar is right but the unit of verdict
  is wrong — one score can't carry the substance, or tiles should
  merge. -> Decomposition (or consolidation) entry; substance
  carries verbatim, never re-litigated.

Bins are citable against a single element finding, not just the
rollup; the element is named when the dispute is element-scoped.
Verdicts the operator agrees with need no note.
