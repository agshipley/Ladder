## 5. Conflicts and arbitrations (rulings recorded)

C-1 — Planning formality. Practitioner counter-canon (seed-stage minimal planning; one-page sufficiency; learning-by-doing — published inside the canon itself as the Schramm counter-piece paired with Gans/Scott/Stern in HBR package R1803B) vs. the empirical planning-performance literature (Schwenk & Shrader 1993; Miller & Cardinal 1994; Brinckmann et al. 2010 moderating). ARBITRATION (operator-ruled 2026-07-09, approved): score existence, articulation, and use of strategic content at a stage-honest bar — a used one-pager renders complete — and demote the formality/depth debate entirely to a dated context annotation for the generation layer. Corpus-verifiable test: artifact exists with use evidence.

C-2 — Mission-statement value. Mission-content research (Pearce & David line: component-rich statements correlate with performance, implying a quality checklist) vs. the founding lineage's own warning that the statement is not the substance — understanding is, and a company can hold a core ideology it never publishes. ARBITRATION (operator-ruled 2026-07-09, approved): score articulated existence and use; never score statement quality against a component checklist. Component research renders as generation-layer context for drafting missions.
