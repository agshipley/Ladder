## 1. Section 2A pre-step record

### 1.1 Scrutiny regimes and lifecycle stages
Four regimes at distinct moments: (1) tax regulators -- continuous, calendar-driven, the
only unconditional deadlines; (2) investor contractual scrutiny -- activates at each
priced round via IRA information rights, persists as delivery obligations; (3) diligence
scrutiny -- episodic at raises/M&A; (4) the operating regime -- the company's own finance
function running the stack, close, review, planning, and treasury, continuously. The
category's essence lives in regime (4), examined by (1)'s calendar.

### 1.2 Regime polarity (mixed, declared per subcategory)
Operating-polarity, practitioner-primary with named-lineage sweeps executed:
FIN-01, FIN-02, FIN-03, FIN-04, FIN-05.
Statutory-polarity, regulator-primary with per-subcategory regulator sweeps executed:
FIN-06 (IRS; Delaware), FIN-07 (IRS Sec. 6039/3111(f)), FIN-08 (state DORs,
post-Wayfair). Grain for the statutory three: engagement-and-currency (see Orientation).

### 1.3 Taxonomy reflection -- frame families and dispositions
Frames: operating-side -- classical CFO decomposition (controller/FP&A/treasury/tax)
cross-checked against the startup-finance service stack (bookkeeping+close → reporting
→ FP&A → tax → treasury); scrutiny-side -- VC financial-DD section structure;
conduct-based tax-exposure frame -- obligations attaching because the company exists
(DE franchise), earns (income tax), employs (payroll filings), pays (1099), grants ISOs
(Sec. 6039), sells (sales/use nexus), spends on R&D (Sec. 174A/41), has foreign ties
(Form 5471).

Dispositions: bookkeeping/GL/CoA/AP-AR/spend → FIN-01. Close and reconciliation →
FIN-01. Accounting policy (basis; rev rec ASC 606; equity instruments ASC 718/480,
detection level) → FIN-01. Statements, review practice, board/investor pack → FIN-02;
IRA deliverable production side FIN-02, obligation side CAP-07 (B-17). Audit posture →
FIN-02 element (C-2). Budget/plan and BvA → FIN-03 (B-2 conditioned; Vision holds the
final planning-split ruling; Finance-side stress-test held with contractual +
practitioner + demand-side corroboration). Model machinery → FIN-04 (R-3); raise-facing
outputs → Capital CAP-01/CAP-06 (B-17, B-30), cross-referenced. Treasury → FIN-05
(E-1 ruled: own scored subcategory). Income/franchise tax → FIN-06. Information returns,
payroll tax, Sec. 6039/3921 → FIN-07 (B-29). Sales/use nexus → FIN-08. Payroll split
per B-31 (ruled this run, per E-2): payroll-tax compliance → Finance FIN-07; payroll
vendor operating process → Ops OPS-05 (B-18 pattern); compensation content → People

corporate record → Legal; the financial pack multi-homes. Foreign
subsidiaries/transfer pricing/non-US VAT → documented n/a at Band 1; Form 5471

## 4. Conflicts and rulings

C-1 (ruled 2026-07-09): reporting cadence. NVCA-standard quarterly vs. practitioner
monthly vs. negotiated monthly covenants in filed exemplars. Ruling: quarterly is the
Band-1 reference cadence for the reporting pack; an executed covenant's terms govern
where present; the monthly practitioner norm reports as context. Applied in FIN-02.

C-2 (ruled 2026-07-09): audited financials. Investor-side DD templates list audited
statements; early-stage market practice and the NVCA bracket treat audit as negotiated
and commonly waived. Ruling: unaudited is perfectly fine at Band 1; an audit is
required only where the company's own executed documents or regulation require one; its
absence otherwise is affirmatively not a gap. Applied in FIN-02.
