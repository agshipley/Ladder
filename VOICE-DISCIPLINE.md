# Voice Discipline
Status: v1.0 (2026-07-15, operator-ruled)
Governs: all prose in this repo and all customer-facing surfaces.

1. Customer-facing surfaces never carry investor-facing claims.
   Moat language, asset language, defensibility language, thesis
   aphorisms — internal docs only. Customer surfaces describe what
   the product does for the customer, in the customer's register.
2. Foil Check (inherited): avoid negation/antithesis constructions
   ("not X, but Y"; "it's not X, it's Y"; trailing ", not X";
   "rather than"; "less about... more about"). State the positive
   claim directly.
3. Jargon rule: internal shorthand gets its plain meaning at first
   use in every durable document. If a term erases a distinction
   (one label for two different problems), retire the term.
